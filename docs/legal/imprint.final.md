# Imprint — the text that ships

Derived from `imprint.source.md` with two citation updates, confirmed by Manu on 2026-08-17.
Everything else is the supplied wording, unchanged.

| Source | Ships | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `Angaben gemäß § 5 TMG` | `Angaben gemäß § 5 DDG` | The provider identification duty moved from the Telemediengesetz to the Digitale-Dienste-Gesetz on 14 May 2024. Section number and substance are unchanged: what stood in § 5 TMG now stands in § 5 DDG. |
| `… nach den allgemein geltenden Gesetzen gemäß §§ 8 bis 10 des Telemediengesetzes (TMG) bleiben hiervon unberührt.` | `… nach den allgemein geltenden Gesetzen bleiben hiervon unberührt.` | **Not a 1:1 swap.** The DDG did not inherit the TMG's §§ 8–10. Those provisions — Durchleitung, Caching, Hosting — are now Articles 4–6 of the Digital Services Act (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065); DDG § 7 is the German limited-liability hook that points at them. `§§ 8 bis 10 DDG` would cite blocking claims, lists of audiovisual media providers and authority information requests, none of which is the intended provision. The citation is not required in an imprint, so it is dropped rather than replaced — the sentence stays correct and cannot go stale again. If an explicit citation is wanted, `Art. 4 bis 6 der Verordnung (EU) 2022/2065 (Digital Services Act)` is the accurate one. |
